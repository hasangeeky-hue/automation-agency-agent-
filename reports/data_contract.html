How to read the verdicts

Four answers, and only four

● Have The data exists in the engine today. The board is empty only because nothing renders it yet. I build the screen and it fills.
◐ Partial Some fields are real, others are not collected. The board renders what is true and says which columns are absent, rather than guessing them.
○ Agent Needs a collector I can build with wires you already have. No new tool, no new cost. This is the work of stage 2.
▢ Tool Needs a credential or a service you would have to arrange. Named explicitly so you can decide whether it is worth it.

What I would build, in this order

The four collectors

  • 1. Orders. Read orders from the connected shop and store them. Unblocks top sellers, slow movers, lifecycle, revenue by channel, ROAS and most of #bi. No new tool. Highest leverage by a wide margin.
  • 2. The host agent. A tiny reporter on the VPS that posts load, disk, uptime and SSL expiry, exactly like the backup receipt. Fills the infra boards that a container structurally cannot see. No new tool.
  • 3. The Data Steward. Code-only, $0 to run. Fills entities, unmapped fields and distribution, and it is the desk that keeps every other number honest as sources multiply.
  • 4. The A/B split. A controlled subject-line test so abTest shows a real result rather than a remembered winner.

What I will not build without you saying so: the Developer desk. Your own spec lists it under "not building, say no honestly", and I agree with that line.

Your decisions

What only you can settle

  • Google Ads OAuth. Media is entirely blocked on it.
  • Backlink data (Ahrefs, DataForSEO or similar). Without it your Link Builder stays an inspector and four link boards stay empty.
  • An ESP for newsletter and campaign routing, or we drop those boards.
  • Competitor ad library and Google Business Profile. Both are real APIs; both cost you time to set up.
  • Amazon, Shopware, TikTok Shop, Snapchat, Pinterest. Six channel dashboards with no wire. Tell me which you actually sell on and I will drop the rest from the OS rather than carry empty frames.
